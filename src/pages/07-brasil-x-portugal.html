Same language, different music

Brazilians and Portuguese understand each other, and your everyday contact with Portugal helps your vocabulary and reading. But in sound, a few rules are almost opposite. Buttons marked 🔊 play the Brazilian voice, buttons marked 🇵🇹 play a European voice if your device has one.

1. Pronúncia · Pronunciation

🇩🇪 Your advantage and your trap

Advantage: the European “r” ([ʁ], uvular) is exactly the German “r”, and a Brazilian initial R is close to German “ch” in “Bach” or even the same sound. Trap: if you copy the European way of swallowing vowels, you will sound Portuguese. In Brazil keep every vowel clear and open: telefone = “te-le-fô-ni”, not “tlfón”.

6. Treine o ouvido · Train your ear

  • Watch series, films and YouTube in Brazilian Portuguese with Portuguese subtitles, and repeat short lines.
  • Listen to Brazilian music (samba, bossa nova, MPB, sertanejo) and read the lyrics from a licensed lyrics site.
  • Choose Brazilian podcasts and voice messages from Brazilian friends: real speed is faster and more connected.
  • Record yourself reading Marc’s text from Apresentação and compare it with the Brazilian audio.
Para a professora · For the teacher

Combine com o Marc uma regra de ouro: “palavras do dia a dia do Brasil”. Quando ele disser uma palavra de Portugal (autocarro, telemóvel), você apenas devolve a palavra brasileira, sem corrigir de forma pesada. Reforce o tema das “palavras perigosas” com humor. Pergunte em qual região do Brasil ele pretende morar para ajustar sotaque e gírias.
