Why Brazilian Portuguese?

About four out of five Portuguese speakers live in Brazil, so this is the version you will hear in most of the Portuguese-speaking world. The written language is nearly the same as in Portugal; the sound is what changes most, and that is what we will train.

1. Você já sabe português! · You already know Portuguese!

Many words look like English or German. Listen, then match each word with its meaning.

Falsos amigos · False friends

Some look familiar but trick you: puxar = to pull (not push) · pasta = folder (also paste) · atual = current · livraria = bookstore · parente = relative · esquisito = weird.

🇩🇪 Obrigado ou obrigada?

A man says obrigado, a woman says obrigada. It follows the speaker, not the listener.

Mini-diálogo · Mini dialogue

Put the words in order to rebuild the dialogue lines.

Para a professora · For the teacher

Faça o diálogo duas vezes: primeiro você como A, depois invertam. Troque “Marc” por outros nomes e inclua “prazer” com um aperto de mão. No Brasil, “Oi, tudo bem?” é resposta e pergunta ao mesmo tempo (“Tudo bem, e você?”).

Run the dialogue twice: first you as A, then swap roles. Replace “Marc” with other names and add “prazer” with a handshake. In Brazil “Oi, tudo bem?” works as both greeting and question (“Tudo bem, e você?”).
